Structure first.
Pixels second.

I like the part of product design where you turn a complicated problem into something that feels almost obvious.

01 · Find the real problem.

Before the interface, I want to understand the person, the task and the constraints around it.

Research · framing · questions

02 · Make the flow click.

Good UI starts with a good path. I map the important decisions before polishing the surface.

Flows · IA · prototypes

03 · Give the system a rhythm.

Patterns should feel related without making every screen look identical.

Components · states · handoff

04 · Sweat the tiny stuff.

Spacing, empty states, edge cases, copy and the moments between screens matter.

Detail · polish · iteration

A little about me

I like figuring things out.

I’m a product designer working across business software, learning platforms and digital products. I enjoy projects where the requirements are a little messy and the interface has to bring some order to them.

I care about the work between Figma and the real product: the flows, states, handoff, edge cases and all the tiny decisions that make a screen feel right.
