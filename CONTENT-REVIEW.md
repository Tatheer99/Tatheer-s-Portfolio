## Add these details to each story

1. **User and problem:** who needed the product, what they were trying to do, and what made that difficult.
2. **Your contribution:** the exact part you owned, collaborators and the scope of the engagement.
3. **One meaningful decision:** the alternatives, what you chose and the evidence or constraint behind the choice.
4. **A real limitation:** something that remained unresolved, a compromise you made or a direction you would change.
5. **Evidence:** readable screens, a focused flow, feedback you can share, or measured results where they actually exist.

Do not add a participant count, business metric, usability improvement or launch status unless you can support it. Design delivery is a valid outcome when business results are unavailable.
